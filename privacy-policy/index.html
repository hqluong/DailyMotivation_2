Privacy Policy for Daily Motivation: Quotes

Developer: Hung Luong  •  Contact: luohung1512@icloud.com

Daily Motivation: Quotes (“the App”) is designed to provide daily inspirational quotes and affirmations. We respect your privacy and built the App to work without collecting personal data from you.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The App does not create user accounts and does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

On-Device Data (stored locally)

The following data is stored only on your device using system storage (UserDefaults) and is not sent to our servers:

  • Preferences and settings (e.g., themes, fonts, backgrounds, selected categories)
  • Reminder times and notification preferences
  • Favorites (quotes you mark as favorite)
  • Quote notes, Daily Reset reflections, and selected small actions
  • Streak and engagement history
  • On-device category preference scores based on saved and skipped quotes

Notifications

The App may schedule local notifications at times you choose. These are created and managed on your device. No device tokens or remote push services are used.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data remains on your device until you remove it.

You can remove favorites, notes, Daily Resets, streaks, preferences, and reminders by choosing “Reset all local data” in the App’s settings. Deleting the App also removes its locally stored data.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us at luohung1512@icloud.com.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. We will update the “Effective date” above. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: luohung1512@icloud.com.
