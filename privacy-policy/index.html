Privacy Policy for Daily Motivation: Quotes

Developer: Hung Luong  •  Contact: trample_pies_4l@icloud.com

Daily Motivation: Quotes (“the App”) is designed to provide daily inspirational quotes and affirmations. We respect your privacy. Your favorites, notes and reflections stay on your device. The App shows ads and uses Google services to measure usage and fix crashes, as described below.

Information We Collect

The App does not create user accounts and we do not sell personal data. The App uses the following Google services, which collect limited information automatically:

  • Google AdMob (ads): device and advertising identifiers (such as Apple’s IDFA, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from IP address, device information, and ad interactions, used to show and measure ads.
  • Firebase Analytics (usage): app-usage events such as opening the App, viewing or saving a quote, sharing, and completing a Daily Reset, along with device information and an app instance identifier. It does not include your notes or reflections.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (diagnostics): crash reports, including device state and stack traces, used to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config: an app instance identifier used to deliver settings such as how often ads appear.

Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your Choices

  • Tracking: the App asks for permission before using your device’s advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are less personalized.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): you’re asked for consent through Google’s consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in the App under Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Usage and crash data: turn off “Share usage and crash data” in the App’s Settings to stop Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection.

On-Device Data (stored locally)

The following data is stored only on your device using system storage (UserDefaults). It is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Preferences and settings (e.g., themes, fonts, backgrounds, selected categories)
  • Reminder times and notification preferences
  • Favorites (quotes you mark as favorite)
  • Quote notes, Daily Reset reflections, and selected small actions
  • Streak and engagement history
  • On-device category preference scores based on saved and skipped quotes

Notifications

The App may schedule local notifications at times you choose. These are created and managed on your device. No device tokens or remote push services are used.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The information described under “Information We Collect” is shared with Google (AdMob and Firebase) as service providers so the App can show ads, measure usage and fix crashes. Your on-device data is not shared.

Data Retention and Deletion

On-device data remains on your device until you remove it. Information collected by Google services is kept according to Google’s retention settings.

You can remove favorites, notes, Daily Resets, streaks, preferences, and reminders by choosing “Reset all local data” in the App’s settings. This also resets the App’s analytics identifier. Deleting the App also removes its locally stored data.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. We will update the “Effective date” above. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

Contact
